# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: October 4, 2026  
**Last Updated**: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our WhatsApp Customer Support AI Agent collects, processes, and safeguards your information in compliance with the **WhatsApp Business Platform Policies** and applicable data privacy regulations.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

1. **WhatsApp Account Identifier (`wa_id`)**: Your phone number as registered on WhatsApp, used solely to route incoming and outgoing messages.
2. **Conversation Transcripts**: Text messages, image attachments, and media sent during support sessions to fulfill your customer inquiries.
3. **Operational Metadata**: Timestamps, delivery status, and tool execution logs.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide automated and human-assisted customer support via WhatsApp.
- To look up order fulfillment and delivery statuses requested by you.
- To schedule appointments and facilitate service callbacks.
- To comply with Meta WhatsApp Cloud API verification and opt-out regulations.

---

## 3. Data Protection & Security

- **Encryption**: All data in transit between Meta and our servers is secured via HTTPS/TLS 1.3 and verified via HMAC-SHA256 signatures.
- **PII Masking**: Customer phone numbers are masked in internal server logs (`+15 ••••• 4567`) to prevent unauthorized exposure.
- **Access Control**: Only authorized staff agents have access to conversation history via password-protected, role-based dashboards.

---

## 4. User Opt-Out ("STOP" Policy)

In accordance with WhatsApp Business Messaging policy:
- You may opt out of automated messages at any time by texting **STOP**, **UNSUBSCRIBE**, or **CANCEL**.
- Once received, our system immediately flags your account and ceases all automated messages.
- You can resume messaging at any time by sending **START**.

---

## 5. Contact Us

If you have questions or wish to request data deletion, contact our privacy officer at `privacy@company.com`.
